Rewilding The Future

A future divided

Imagine...

“Set aside the right half of the planet, with careful attention to which habitats and which species most need protection, and we can save the great majority of the life that remains. This is not a romantic notion. It is a biological reality, supported by decades of research across hundreds of ecosystems.” “To be human is to love the natural world. That love is not a luxury or an eccentricity. It is the foundation of our sanity.”
“Children who grow up in environments impoverished of other species are likely to grow up psychologically impoverished as well. The atrophy of biophilia is a form of mental illness — quiet, chronic, and largely unrecognised.”
The situation facing us is unprecedented. Never before in the history of life on Earth has a single species held the fate of nearly all other species in its hands. That species is us. The choice we make now — or fail to make — will determine the living world for millions of years to come.”
“Imagine a world in which humanity has done the right thing. Half the planet is in reserve, the remainder managed wisely. Biodiversity loss has been halted. The great animal migrations continue. The Amazon remains intact. The Serengeti teems. The deep ocean is mapped and protected. Our descendants live in a world richer in life than any human generation before us has known.”
